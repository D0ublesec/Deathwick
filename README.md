# Deathwick

A candlelit card game of survival and betrayal. You and your neighbours sit in a ritual circle, each guarding a Candle: your deck of cards and your life. Every turn you must Burn cards to feed the Ghosts haunting your Shadow, then take one action: Haunt a neighbour, Banish a ghost, or Cast a spell. Empty your Candle and you're Consumed; let three Ghosts of the same suit possess you and you're lost. Choose a class, play your neighbours, and be the last light burning.

**Players:** 2–8 per ritual circle (split larger groups into separate circles - see the manual).

**Play:**
- **Physical** - Any standard deck(s) + Jokers; rules and class selection at [manual.html](manual.html).
- **Digital** - Pass-and-play on one device at [game.html](game.html) (not online multiplayer).
