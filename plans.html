Plans & Intentions

Why Deathwick exists and where it’s headed

Why this game exists

I wanted a fun and strategic game that can be played with a simple deck of playing cards, whether that’s two of you at home or a larger group split across ritual circles while travelling.

That way it’s accessible to anyone who has cards. You’re not forced to buy yet another boxed game. Grab a standard deck (or two, depending on player count), learn the rules from this site, and play.

And because I cannot let the spook of Halloween wait for one moment in the year, Deathwick is here so the candle can flicker whenever you need it.

How you can play (now and later)

  • With your own cards - Use the manual and any standard playing cards. No purchase required. New players: start at Start here in the manual.
  • Browser game (now) - Play game is pass-and-play on one device (up to 8 per circle) - hand the phone or laptop around the table; it is not online multiplayer.
  • Physical copy (planned) - For those who want to feel more immersed, I’d like to get the game printed so people can buy a physical copy with dedicated art and components.
  • Steam & mobile (planned) - A proper Steam and/or mobile release would aim to add online multiplayer and deliver a more polished digital experience.
  • Tabletop Simulator - I’m also creating Deathwick in Tabletop Simulator for people who prefer playing that way online with friends.
